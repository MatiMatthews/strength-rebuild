# Weekly targets

A completed week can propose changes for each exercise in the next unstarted week. The review shows sets, reps, load and the reason for each change or hold. Accept applies the displayed, verified changes together. Keep and reject close the review without changing the plan.

The calculation uses completed workouts and audited history corrections. Missing or failed work, unknown loads, incompatible prescriptions, restrictions and power exercises hold their targets. A previously accepted session recommendation is preserved to prevent duplicate progression. Changed evidence, settings, safety or started work invalidates an open proposal; keep or reject remains available.

Original plan and workout snapshots remain unchanged. Accepted weekly adjustments are portable audit records used when opening the plan and the next workout. Encrypted restore validates these records before replacing local data.

Historical reviews and incomplete, failed, repeated or restricted outcomes still close conservatively without target adjustments. Repaired prescriptions remain explicitly unavailable for weekly adjustment. Final-week review does not create another week or activate another cycle.
