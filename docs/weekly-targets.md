# Weekly targets

A completed week can propose changes for each exercise in the next unstarted week. The review shows sets, reps, load and the reason for each change or hold. Accept applies the displayed, verified changes together. Keep and reject close the review without changing the plan.

The calculation uses completed workouts and audited history corrections. Unknown loads, incompatible or missing evidence, restrictions and power exercises hold their targets. In recovery review, verified missed sets, insufficient repetitions or effort can support the existing five-percent load reduction for the affected exercise. Other exercises retain their prescriptions. Repeated failures that call for repeating a week are explained as a hold; the review does not create extra weeks. A previously accepted session recommendation is preserved to prevent duplicate progression. Changed evidence, settings, safety or started work invalidates an open proposal; keep or reject remains available.

Original plan and workout snapshots remain unchanged. Accepted weekly adjustments are portable audit records used when opening the plan and the next workout. Encrypted restore validates these records before replacing local data.

A reported incomplete, failed or repeated outcome alone cannot authorize a reduction. Historical reviews retain their original inputs and output; a separate explicit preview is required before newly calculated targets can be accepted. Restricted outcomes close conservatively without target adjustments. Repaired prescriptions remain explicitly unavailable for weekly adjustment. Final-week review does not create another week or activate another cycle.
